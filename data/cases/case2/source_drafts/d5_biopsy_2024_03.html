Clinical History

52F with new 1.2 cm spiculated mass in right breast at 11 o'clock, 3 cm from nipple, identified on screening mammogram dated 02/22/2024. BI-RADS 4. Image-guided core biopsy performed for tissue diagnosis.

Specimen

A) Right breast mass, 11 o'clock position, ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy. Submitted in formalin.

Gross Description

Received in formalin labeled with patient identifiers and "right breast 11 o'clock". Six (6) tan-white, fibrous-appearing cylindrical cores ranging from 1.0 to 1.6 cm in length, 0.1 cm in diameter. All submitted in cassette A1.

Microscopic Description

Sections show benign breast parenchyma with a circumscribed fibroepithelial proliferation. The lesion demonstrates a biphasic pattern of compressed glandular epithelium and an expanded, cellular stromal component. Stromal cellularity is mild to moderate without atypia, and there are no mitotic figures. Epithelial elements are bland with no features of atypical hyperplasia, ductal carcinoma in situ, or invasive carcinoma. No necrosis, no calcifications.

Immunohistochemical stains were not indicated given the morphologic features.

Diagnosis

Pathology: Right breast mass, ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy. DIAGNOSIS: Fibroadenoma, no evidence of malignancy.

Comment

Findings are concordant with the imaging description of a circumscribed mass in a peri-menopausal patient. Recommend routine clinical follow-up per referring surgeon.
