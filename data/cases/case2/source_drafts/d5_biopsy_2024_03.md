fibroepithelial proliferation. The lesion demonstrates a biphasic pattern

features of atypical hyperplasia, ductal carcinoma in situ, or invasive
carcinoma. No necrosis, no calcifications.

Immunohistochemical stains were not indicated given the morphologic
